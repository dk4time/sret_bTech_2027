a) Users and vehicles

Hierarchical inheritance (User → Customer / Captain), multilevel inheritance (Vehicle → Cab → CabEconomy / CabPremium), composition (Captain ◆ Vehicle), and enums with fields. Source: docs/diagrams/a_users_and_vehicles.mmd

b) Ride and payments

rendering…

Ride guards its own status (no setStatus). It aggregates Customer and Captain, and is composed of its request, receipt and timeline. Payable is realised by three payment classes (dynamic dispatch). Source: docs/diagrams/b_ride_and_payments.mmd
