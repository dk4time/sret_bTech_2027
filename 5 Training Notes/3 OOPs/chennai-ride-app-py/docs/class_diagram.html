(a) Users and vehicles

The two hierarchies of the model. User is abstract with two children (hierarchical inheritance). Vehicle → Cab → CabEconomy / CabPremium is multilevel inheritance. A Captain owns exactly one Vehicle (composition ◆). Location and Money are immutable value objects.

(b) Ride and payments

Ride guards its own status and hides the OTP in the name-mangled __otp (the only - private member in the project). It refers to an existing Customer and Captain (aggregation ◇) and owns its request, estimate and receipt (composition ◆). Payable is an interface realised by UPI, Cash and Wallet (dashed arrows).

(c) Services and notifications

ChennaiRideApp is the composition root: it creates every service. Services receive the services they need through their constructors, which is why those links are aggregation ◇ (shared). RideService holds a list[Notifier] and calls notify() on each, which is polymorphism.

(d) CLI layer

Menu is abstract and its run() loop is written once. Six menus inherit it. MainMenu builds its four sub-menus and holds them through the Menu type, which is polymorphism. The CLI has no business rules: CliSession owns the same ChennaiRideApp world that the scripted demo uses.
